This Privacy Policy explains how {{brandName}} ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal
information when you use {{siteUrl}} and our services. {{brandName}} is an online service operated by
{{operatorName}}. We do not have a physical office open to the public. You can reach us at

By using {{brandName}} you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** Your email address, your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google
or GitHub, and the sign-in method you used. We do not use passwords for regular accounts.

**Your project content.** The ideas, answers and brand details you enter, text, links or documents you
paste or upload, photos and videos you upload, the websites we generate for you and their versions,
research results for your projects, and your chat messages about a project.

**Payment information.** Payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive the order and payment IDs, the
status, the amount and the plan you bought. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

**Usage and technical information.** Your IP address, browser and device information, pages and actions
in the app, error logs, and your credit activity. We use this to run the service, keep it secure and
prevent abuse.

**Cookies.** We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to protect sign-in and sign-up with bot
checks (Cloudflare Turnstile). We do not use cookies for advertising. If we add analytics in the future,
we will update this policy.

**Public websites.** We visit publicly available competitor websites to learn about their design. This
does not involve your personal data.

## 2. How we use your information

- To provide the service: research, website generation, images and videos, previews, edits and hosting.
- To process payments, add credits to your account and keep a record of your purchases.
- To send sign-in links, receipts and important service messages.
- To keep the service safe: detect fraud, abuse and automated sign-ups.
- To give support and answer your requests.
- To improve the product using aggregated or de-identified usage information.
- To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. AI processing

To create your results we send the information needed for each task to AI model providers. This
includes the text you entered, extracted details from your documents or links, and images you uploaded
when they are needed for the task. Depending on the task and the model you choose, these providers are
Anthropic, DeepSeek, OpenAI and Moonshot AI (Kimi). Search and ranking providers (Voyage AI, Cohere)
and Brave Search receive text derived from your project description to find relevant research and
similar websites. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy. Please do not
enter sensitive personal data, such as government ID numbers, health records or financial account
numbers, into your projects.

Generated results can be inaccurate. You are responsible for checking them before you publish them.

## 4. Who else receives your information

We use trusted service providers to run {{brandName}}. They only receive what they need to do their job:

- **Payments:** Razorpay.
- **Email delivery:** Resend.
- **Hosting and infrastructure:** Google Cloud (our servers), Vercel (this website), Neon (our
  database), Upstash (temporary data and job queues) and Cloudflare (file storage and the hosting of
  your published sites).
- **Website previews:** StackBlitz, whose WebContainers technology runs the preview of your site in your
  browser.
- **AI, search and ranking providers:** as listed in section 3.
- **Security and fraud prevention:** Cloudflare (Turnstile bot checks).

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights and users, or as part of a
business transfer, in which case we will tell you.

## 5. Websites you publish

When you deploy a website, it becomes public on the internet. Anything you put on it, including text,
images, videos and contact details, can be seen by anyone. Do not publish information you do not want
to be public.

## 6. How long we keep information

We keep your account and project information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your
account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable time, normally within 30
days, except records we must keep by law, such as payment and tax records. Backups are removed on their
normal cycle.

## 7. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and monitoring to protect your information. No online
service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your rights

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to
give you access to your personal data, correct it, delete it, or withdraw your consent. You can also
nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you are unable to. To make a request, email
{{supportEmail}} from the email address on your account. We will respond within the time the law requires.

## 9. Children

{{brandName}} is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Transfers outside India

Some of our service providers process data in other countries. By using the service you understand that
your information may be processed outside India, with the protections described in this policy.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for important changes, tell you
by email or in the app.
