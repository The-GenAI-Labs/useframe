**All purchases on {{brandName}} are final. We do not offer refunds.** Please read this page before you
buy.

## 1. What you are buying

You buy one-time credit packs. Credits are digital, are added to your account right after payment, and
are used by actions in the app such as research, website generation, images, videos and hosting. There is
no subscription and nothing renews automatically, so there is nothing to cancel.

## 2. No refunds

Because credits are digital and are available to use immediately, we do not refund purchases. This
includes unused credits, a change of mind, not being happy with an AI-generated result, a project you
stopped, and deleting your account.

## 3. Billing mistakes we will fix

We will review and, if confirmed, correct the following:

- You were charged more than once for the same order.
- Money was deducted but credits were not added to your account within 24 hours.
- A payment was made without your authorisation.

To ask, email {{supportEmail}} within 7 days of the payment, from your account email, with the Razorpay
payment ID or a screenshot of the payment. We reply within {{responseTime}}. If we approve it, we refund
the amount to the original payment method. Your bank or Razorpay usually needs 5 to 7 business days to
show it. In some cases we may add the credits instead.

## 4. Failed actions

If a credit-using action fails because of an error on our side, the credits reserved for it are returned
to your balance automatically. This is not a refund of money.

## 5. Cancelling and closing your account

You can stop using {{brandName}} at any time. You can ask us to delete your account at {{supportEmail}}.
Unused credits are lost when an account is deleted and are not refunded.

## 6. Chargebacks

If you have a problem with a payment, please contact us first. We will work with you to solve it. Opening
a dispute with your bank for a valid purchase may lead to your account being suspended while we review it.
