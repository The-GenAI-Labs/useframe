{{brandName}} is a fully digital, online-only service. We do not sell or ship physical goods, so there is
no shipping, courier or physical delivery.

## How delivery works

- **Credits:** after your payment is confirmed, credits are added to your {{brandName}} account
  immediately, usually within a few minutes. You will see the new balance in the app.
- **Websites, images and videos:** these are created in your account when you run an action. Time depends
  on the task. Larger jobs, such as videos, take longer.
- **Published sites:** when you deploy, your site goes live on your {{brandName}} subdomain or your own
  domain. A custom domain can take time to start working because of DNS changes made by your domain
  provider.

## If something does not arrive

If money was deducted but your credits are not in your account after 24 hours, email {{supportEmail}}
with your account email and the payment ID. We reply within {{responseTime}}. See our Refund Policy for
how billing mistakes are handled.
